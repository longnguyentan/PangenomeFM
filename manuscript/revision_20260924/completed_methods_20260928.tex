\subsection{Segment-grouped masked sequence-feature development}

The sequence-conditioned encoder $E$ uses the native 48-dimensional, two-layer, four-head backbone with bidirectional graph messages and unchanged frozen NT input vectors. We mask 30\% of biological segment IDs, replacing all 519 input columns at both oriented handles with a learned token. Targets are 512-dimensional NT vectors standardized using unique training segments only. A linear projection, zeroing of masked latent rows, one graph-attention decoder layer and a linear output reconstruct these targets with scaled cosine error (exponent two). The full adjacency remains visible because edges are not prediction targets; explicit position/orientation covariates also remain. This is an adaptation of an established masked-autoencoding objective, not a reproduction of the official GraphMAE architecture or a claim of a novel general objective.

The four arms per seed are full trained, full frozen random, coordinate-only trained and coordinate-only frozen random. Trained/random pairs start from identical encoder weights; random backbones must remain unchanged even while the decoder is optimized. All arms use the original fold-A one-hop eligible windows, three seeds, a 20-epoch ceiling and patience five; checkpoint selection minimizes self-supervised validation loss. Unique training and validation segment sets do not overlap. Frozen cCRE and historical INS/DEL probes use $C+S$, $C+S+E$, $C+S+H$ and $C+S+H+E$, validation chromosomes only, and a uniform 4,000-iteration cap. Neither NT nor the graph encoder receives biological-label gradients. The development gate requires a positive full-trained-minus-full-random AP increment after $H$ for both tasks at every seed; coordinate-only contrasts are reported separately. No initialization-seed interval is treated as genomic uncertainty.

The pending chromosome protocol retains all four arms and all three seeds on the five original folds. It prespecifies an A/C/D/E primary summary and separate fold-B and all-fold development-exposed sensitivities. The separately recorded original-v1 comparison uses the same probe iteration ceiling. Neither planned matrix is summarized before complete, audited execution. A separate whole-graph NT extension covers all 751,237 segments and preserves all 479,477 benchmark rows byte-for-byte; the fixed replication continues to use its original benchmark cache. Cache completion and wider-context preparation alone are not biological performance results.
